1 · the apps

Five apps, one switcher in the top bar

Today's Zorro Settings splits in two. DEVS, Intelligence and Connect are new. Every app has the same bar at the top to jump between the five. Click a name to see what it holds.

Also: a clearer order inside the apps

The pages get regrouped while splitting, so each app reads top to bottom in the order you use it. I'll show the new order here before building.

2 · the dialer

Seven parts around the clock

Apps, Music and Notes stay. AI is new: a chat with a more regular layout, in the same look. Messages and notifications become one part. System and Files stay.

new

AI

A chat: type a question, answers below, a short history. Same black, thin lines and font as the rest of the Dialer. Which engine answers is your choice (see AI).

merged

Messages & notifications

SMS and other messages together with the desktop notifications, in one part.

3 · music output

Pick where the music plays, in one tap

In the Dialer's Music part: your audio Bluetooth devices, greyed when not connected. One tap connects the device and plays through it. In System → Bluetooth, a checkbox per device decides whether it gets this shortcut.

4 · ai

One AI service, Claude first, Mistral as fallback

The Dialer, Intelligence and DEVS all ask one small background service. It sends the question to Claude, or to Mistral when Claude can't be reached. Per place, you choose which one is used.

5 · how we build

A code review before and after

The build is wrapped in two reviews, so we start from clean code and end with checked code.

step 1

Review before

A review of the code the build will change: Zorro Settings (zsettings), the Dialer (zorro-dial), Transfer and this session's commits. Bugs found get fixed first, or go into DEVS.

step 2

Build

In small steps, each tested in a private test screen and the VM, committed and pushed.

step 3

Review after

A review of everything the build changed, before you take it into use. Findings are fixed or listed.

The findings of both reviews will show on this page.
